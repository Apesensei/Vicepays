Vice Pays

8 or more of the same symbol anywhere on the 6 by 5 grid pay. Winning symbols drop away and new ones fall in, for as long as wins keep coming.

Heat signs stay on the grid until the drops stop. If the spin won, their values are added together and multiply the win.

Wild: counts as every symbol and is used up by the win it joins. Wilds only arrive by drive-by.

Random events in the base game: a drive-by throws 2 to 5 wilds onto the grid; an upgrade turns every copy of the commonest low symbol into one high symbol; a monitor glitch can drop a fourth police light in when exactly 3 are showing.

The Getaway: 4 or more police lights start 15 free spins. Heat from every winning free spin is banked into Total Heat, which multiplies that spin and never resets. 3 or more police lights during the Getaway add 5 spins.

Demo only: play money, no real wagering. Maximum win 10,000x. Return to player .
